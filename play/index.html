How to play

Goal: Haul cargo across the lane and get paid.

Controls

  • WASD move
  • Space fire
  • ↑ bomb, if you are carrying one
  • ↓ cone beam, if you own it
  • P / Esc pause
  • M mute
  • 9 this tab

Mobile: drag to fly. Hold FIRE to shoot. Tap BEAM for the cone beam if you own it. A bomb you carry sits above FIRE.

The rules

  • On a fragile contract, each hull hit cuts that contract's pay. A shield hit does not.
  • If the hull is destroyed, that crate pays nothing. Credits already in the bank stay.
  • Spend credits in the Yard shop.
  • The Threat Log fills in the first time you meet an enemy.
  • Black Contracts pay 1,600. A patrol tag is an arrest and a 400 credit fine.
